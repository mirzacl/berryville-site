A cosy restaurant game, made of berries

Customers order, your cooks walk to the bench, make the dish and carry it over, and every delivery pays. Upgrade your recipes, hire a team, and grow from a street stand to a busy bistro. Everything on the menu is made with berries: smoothies, pancakes, waffles, pies and froyo.

Seven restaurants

From a stand to a bistro

Every city has seven restaurants: a stand, a bigger stand, a food truck, a café, a diner, a drive-thru and a sit-down bistro with waiters. Each one brings something new: tips, specials, drive-thru cars, table service and more.

The Grand Tour

Eleven cities to visit

Move your business from BerryVille to the seaside of Raspberry Bay and on through the Grand Tour. Further along, cities bring landmarks and twists of their own, and your passport fills with stamps as you go.

The dressing room

Dress your chef

Over a hundred pieces to find: hats, aprons, tools, pins and lockets, in full sets with their own trims. Every piece you wear helps your restaurants sell.

Ten pals

Little helpers with big jobs

Hatch pals from eggs and watch them work: a bee who brings golden guests, a hedgehog who tops dishes with blackberries, a mouse who polishes the tip jar. They grow from Sprout to Bloom to Ripe.

Questions

When can I play?

BerryVille is coming to the App Store for iPhone soon. This page will have the link the day it is out.

Will it be free?

Yes, BerryVille will be free to download.

Is it coming to Android?

iPhone comes first. If players enjoy it, an Android version is the next step.

Do I need an account or the internet?

No. There is no account, and the game plays without an internet connection. Your progress is saved on your iPhone.

How do I send feedback?

In the game, open Settings and tap SEND FEEDBACK, or write to contact@berryville.app. Every message is read.
